# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI**.
The dashboard analyzes coffee shop sales performance across products, product categories, store locations, and different time periods. It helps transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights for better decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objective
The main objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales data and identify:
- Overall revenue and sales performance
- Total orders and quantity sold
- Best and worst-performing products
- Product category performance
- Store-level performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Weekday vs weekend sales
- Daily sales performance
- Hourly sales trends
- Store contribution to total revenue

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides an overall summary of coffee shop sales performance.

## 📈 Key Insights
Based on the dashboard analysis:
- The coffee shop generated approximately **$699K in total revenue**.
- Approximately **149K orders** were recorded.
- Total quantity sold was approximately **214K units**.
- The average order sale was approximately **$4.69**.
- Revenue showed a strong upward trend from January to June.
- **Coffee** generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- The three store locations generated relatively similar revenue.
- Weekdays generated significantly more quantity sold than weekends.
- Sales varied throughout the day, helping identify peak selling hours.
- A small group of products contributed significantly to total revenue.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard insights, the following actions can be considered:
1. Focus marketing campaigns on high-performing products.
2. Analyze low-performing products and consider promotional strategies.
3. Increase inventory for products with consistently high demand.
4. Optimize staff allocation during peak sales hours.
5. Use monthly sales trends for inventory and workforce planning.
6. Compare store performance regularly to identify improvement opportunities.
7. Develop targeted promotions for lower-performing products.
8. Monitor product category contribution to maximize revenue.
